- **FAILURE IS THE NORM, NOT THE EXCEPTION**
- A cloud is basically a Geo-distributed system over a network.
- A distributed system is just a number of computers cooperating together and communicating among each other.
- Every set of connected computers and resources is called a **Grid** 
- It could be data-driven or computation-driven (most systems today are data-driven)
- Cloud systems have to emphasize 4 main qualities:
	1. **Fault-Tolerance** (including Availability & Recovery & Replication)
	2. **Correctness** (trades off Performance back and forth)
	3. **Performance** (probably through Parallelism and Scalability)
	4. **Security** (beyond the scope of this course)
	
- The main infrastructure of distributed systems are based on:
	1. **Data Storage** (and Retrieval)
	2. **Communication** (between nodes)
	3. **Computation** (including Load Balancing)
